## 1. Pistas de Contexto para Inferencia (Who / What / Where — Skill 4)

El TOEFL evalúa constantemente la capacidad de inferir la ocupación de una persona (*Who*), la acción subsiguiente (*What next*) o la ubicación (*Where*) a través de palabras clave (*context clues*):

| Dominio | Pistas en el Diálogo (*Clues*) | Inferencia Directa (*Target*) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Who** | *your class, homework assignment, quiz* | **Teacher** (Profesor) |
| **Who** | *deposit this check, cash back* | **Bank teller** (Cajero de banco) |
| **Who** | *prescription, medicine, pills, dosage* | **Pharmacist** (Farmacéutico) |
| **Who** | *cavity, filling, toothache, checkup* | **Dentist** (Dentista) |
| **Who** | *sole, heel, shoe repair* | **Shoe repairperson** |
| **Who** | *wings, cockpit, flight, takeoff* | **Pilot / Flight attendant** |
| **What Next**| *check out books, take them home* | **Go to the circulation desk** (Mostrador de préstamo) |
| **What Next**| *paycheck, deposit, list of errands* | **Go to the bank** |
| **What Next**| *stand in line at the checkout counter* | **Pay for groceries** (Pagar en caja) |
| **What Next**| *put on some suntan lotion, go into the water* | **Go swimming / Sunbathe at the beach** |
| **Where** | *water, sand, suntan lotion, waves* | **At the beach** (En la playa) |
| **Where** | *checkout counter, bread, eggs, milk, groceries* | **In a market / Supermarket** |
| **Where** | *darkroom, lens, camera, portrait, print* | **In a photography studio** |
| **Where** | *slide, microscope, specimen, experiment* | **In a biology laboratory** |
| **Where** | *pump gas, check the oil, windshield* | **At a service station** (Gasolinera) |

## 2. Patrones de Transformación Pasiva ↔ Activa (Skill 5)

En Listening Part A, cuando un interlocutor usa una oración en voz activa, la respuesta correcta casi siempre es su paráfrasis exacta en **voz pasiva** (o viceversa):

| Oración Original (Diálogo) | Estructura | Paráfrasis Correcta (Respuesta) | Estructura |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *She got a new checking account.* | Activa | *A new checking account was opened.* | Pasiva |
| *Her tuition has already been paid.* | Pasiva | *Alice has already paid her fees.* | Activa |
| *I watered the lawn only this morning.* | Activa | *The lawn has already been watered today.* | Pasiva |
| *I heard that she was just found!* | Pasiva | *Someone located the girl.* | Activa |
| *The storm damaged the trees.* | Activa | *The trees were damaged by the storm.* | Pasiva |
| *Mac was chosen by his classmates.* | Pasiva | *His classmates selected Mac.* | Activa |
| *The insurance company paid for the damage.* | Activa | *The damage was covered by insurance.* | Pasiva |

## 3. Patrones de Múltiples Sustantivos y Roles (Skill 6)

El examen confunde deliberadamente el orden de los sujetos y objetos con verbos de sustitución, designación o causativos:

| Expresión / Estructura | Significado Real | Trampa Común en Respuestas |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A replaced B** (*Mara replaced Robert in the band*) | **A entra, B sale** (Mara está en la banda; Robert no). | Invertir quién reemplazó a quién (*Robert took Mara's place* ❌). |
| **Doctor made him take time off** | **El paciente descansa por orden médica** (*On doctor's orders*). | Creer que el doctor fue quien se tomó vacaciones (*The doctor took time off* ❌). |
| **Named beneficiary in will** | **Heredero legal** (*Received an inheritance*). | Confundir beneficio genérico con herencia testamentaria. |
| **Baby-sitter** vs **Baby sister** | *Baby-sitter* = Niñera / Cuidadora.<br>*Baby sister* = Hermana menor. | Confusión fonética directa entre palabras compuestas similares. |

## 4. Equivalencias de Negación con Opuestos (Skill 7)

La respuesta correcta a una expresión negativa suele ser una afirmación positiva con un adjetivo o adverbio **antónimo**:

| Expresión Negativa en Diálogo | Antónimo / Significado Equivalente |
| :--- | :--- |
| *didn't drive slowly* | **drove quickly / fast** (manejaron rápido) |
| *not sad* | **happy / pleased** (feliz, satisfecho) |
| *nobody arrived on time* | **everyone was late** (todos llegaron tarde) |
| *never works hard* | **is lazy / avoids work** (es flojo / no se esfuerza) |
| *insane* (*in-* + sane) | **not sane = crazy** (loco, fuera de sí) |
| *isn't the right key* | **is the wrong key** (es la llave incorrecta) |
| *nothing went as planned* | **the event was disorganized / a mess** (fue un desorden) |
| *unsure about the idea* | **hesitant / doubtful** (dubitativo, no convencido) |
| *wasn't too good* | **was poor / mediocre** (fue malo / deficiente) |

## 5. Vocabulario Académico de Lectura (Reading — Life Cycle of Stars)

| Término | Definición en Contexto | Sinónimo en el Examen |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stellar** | Relativo a las estrellas y su física. | *Star-related / Star-filled* |
| **Protostar** | Concentración densa inicial de gas antes de la fusión nuclear. | *Initial stage of a star's cycle* |
| **Diffuse** | Esparcido, no denso, disperso en un volumen grande. | *Not concentrated / Dispersed* |

| **Depleted / Runs out** | Agotamiento del combustible nuclear de hidrógeno. | *Exhausted / Consumed* |
| **Onset** | El inicio o comienzo de una fase crítica o crisis estelar. | *Beginning / Start* |

| **Dregs** | Los últimos residuos o sedimentos finales de combustible. | *Remnants / Leftovers* |
| **Wink out** | Apagarse gradualmente por falta de energía. | *Extinguish / Die out* |
| **Chronicled** | Documentado o registrado de forma secuencial y detallada. | *Recorded / Documented* |
| **White dwarf** | Enana blanca (fase final de estrellas de masa media como el Sol). | *Dense stellar remnant* |
| **Helium flash** | Ignición térmica descontrolada de helio en estrellas de masa media. | *High-energy explosive ignition* |

## 6. Patrones de Doble Negación (Skill 8)

Dos negaciones en la misma cláusula resultan en un significado afirmativo o de posibilidad:

| Patrón de Doble Negación | Ejemplo | Significado Real Equivalente |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Negativo + Prefijo Negativo** (*not + un-/in-/dis-*) | *isn't impossible*<br>*didn't like the unclean office*<br>*no one is unhappy* | **is possible / might happen** (es posible)<br>**liked clean office** (le gustó la oficina limpia)<br>**everyone is pleased / happy** |
| **Dos Verbos Negativos** (*not... not...*) | *didn't study enough, so didn't do well*<br>*isn't snowing, so aren't going* | **poor grade because of inadequate preparation**<br>implica que *irían si estuviera nevando* |
| **Neither / Not... either** | *Mark couldn't get tickets, and neither could Paul.* | **Both were unable to get tickets** (Ninguno de los dos pudo conseguir boletos). |

## 7. Expresiones "Casi Negativas" (*Almost Negative* — Skill 9)

Estas expresiones indican que una afirmación es técnicamente cierta, pero por un margen tan estrecho que **casi no ocurrió**:

| Tipo de Expresión | Disparadores (*Keywords*) | Ejemplo | Significado Real |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Almost none** (Casi nada / Casi ninguno) | *hardly, barely, scarcely, only* | *had barely enough money*<br>*has hardly any experience*<br>*only once has he parked there* | **paid the bill but has no money left**<br>**has not worked for very long**<br>**parked on just one occasion** |
| **Almost never** (Casi nunca) | *rarely, seldom* | *seldom taken so much time*<br>*rarely drives to work* | **has rarely worked so hard**<br>**almost never drives** |

---

## 8. Negativos con Comparativos con Significado Superlativo (Skill 10)

Cuando se combina un elemento negativo (*no, not, never, couldn't*) con un adjetivo comparativo (*more, -er*), la oración tiene fuerza de **superlativo absoluto** (es lo máximo posible):

| Estructura | Ejemplo en Diálogo | Paráfrasis Superlativa (Respuesta) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **No one + more [adjective]** | *No one is more intelligent than she is.* | **She is the smartest student in the class.** |
| **Couldn't be + [-er]** | *I couldn't be feeling any better today.* | **He is feeling terrific / in excellent health.** |
| **Couldn't have been + more [adj]** | *He couldn't have been more unfriendly.* | **He was extremely unfriendly / disliked him.** |
| **No one else + could have done better**| *No one else could have done better.* | **Theresa got the highest grade.** |
| **Couldn't have been + easier** | *The exam couldn't have been easier.* | **The exam was extremely easy.** |
| **Couldn't have been + better** | *The results could not have been better.* | **The results were perfect / outstanding.** |

## 10. Patrones de Error Gramatical Común (Peterson's Practice Test 3)

| Regla Gramatical | Uso Incorrecto (*Error*) | Corrección Estándar (*Correct Form*) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Homófonos / Cláusulas sustantivas** | *I doubt **weather** he will enter...* | *I doubt **whether** he will enter...* |
| **Condicional mixto / Tercer condicional** | *If they had been in better condition, they **might enjoyed**...* | *...they **might have enjoyed** the hike more.* |
| **Modificador colgante (*Dangling modifier*)** | *Not having passed the law exam, **the state** refused...* | *Not having passed the law exam, **he** was refused a license...* |
| **Adverbio modificando participio** | *The **interesting designed** stairway...* | *The **interestingly designed** stairway...* |
| **Formas no estándar de adverbios de lugar** | *find one **anywheres*** | *find one **anywhere*** |
| **Consistencia de tiempos con tiempo pasado puntual** | *I **have reserved** seats ... **last night**.* | *I **reserved** seats ... last night.* |
| **Paralelismo en listas de cursos** | *...audio-lingual, reading, writing, laboratory, and **to choose**...* | *...and **a special-interest** course.* |
| **Doble negación con preposiciones** | *...survive another week **without no** rain.* | *...**without any** rain.* |
| **Determinantes con sustantivos contables singulares** | *The children had **such difficult time**...* | *The children had **such a difficult time**...* |
| **Causativo HAVE con objeto persona** | *He had his tailor **made** an exotic robe...* | *He had his tailor **make** an exotic robe...* |
| **Caso pronominal relativo (*Subject vs Object*)** | *...pass the ball to **whomever** is in the best position...* | *...to **whoever** is in the best position...* (sujeto del verbo *is*). |
| **Preposiciones fijas con verbos (*Depend on*)** | *Jane's decision will depend largely **to** her ability...* | *...depend largely **on / upon** her ability...* |
| **Verbos de enlace (*Linking verbs*) con adjetivos** | *Dr. Alvarez looked **tiredly**...* | *Dr. Alvarez looked **tired**...* |
| **Subjuntivo en cláusulas de importancia/urgencia** | *It is important that you **turned** off the heater...*<br>*It was essential that we **signed** the lease...* | *...that you **turn** off...* (forma base infinitiva)<br>*...that we **sign** the lease...* |
| **Verbos transitivos vs intransitivos (*Raise vs Rise / Lay vs Lie / Set vs Sit*)** | *The cost of gasoline **has raised**...*<br>*I **set** in the wrong chair...*<br>*The doctor advised that Jim **lay** down...* | *The cost ... **has risen**.* (intransitivo)<br>*I **sat** in the wrong chair.* (intransitivo)<br>*...that Jim **lie** down.* (intransitivo/subjuntivo) |

## 11. Expresiones de Acuerdo (*Expressions of Agreement* — Skill 11)

El acuerdo con el interlocutor previo es uno de los temas más frecuentes en Listening Part A:

| Tipo de Declaración Previa | Expresiones de Acuerdo | Significado en Respuestas del Examen |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Declaración Positiva** (*Affirmative*) | *So do I.*<br>*Me, too.*<br>*I'll say!*<br>*Isn't it! / Aren't they!*<br>*You can say that again!* | **Agrees with the speaker / Shares the same opinion / Finds it fascinating, bad, etc.** |
| **Declaración Negativa** (*Negative*) | *Neither do I.*<br>*I don't either.* | **Also dislikes it / Shares negative assessment / Agrees it is not good.** |

## 12. Expresiones de Incertidumbre y Sugerencia (Skill 12)

| Función | Disparadores (*Keywords*) | Ejemplo | Significado Real |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Incertidumbre (*Uncertainty*)** | *Tag questions* (*...isn't it? / ...don't they?*)<br>*As far as I know.*<br>*As far as I can tell.* | *It's going to be difficult, isn't it?*<br>*They have to leave at 4:00, don't they?*<br>*As far as I know.* | **Thinks it might be hard / Not completely sure / Thinks s/he knows.** |
| **Sugerencia (*Suggestion*)** | *Why not ...?*<br>*Let's ...* | *Why not do it now?*<br>*Let's stop and get a drink.* | **Finishing it today / Taking a break and having a drink.** |

## 13. Expresiones Enfáticas de Sorpresa (Skill 13)

El énfasis vocal en el verbo auxiliar o modal (*be, do/does, did, have, modal*) indica que el hablante **suponía o creía lo contrario antes de ese momento**:

| Estructura Enfática | Ejemplo en Diálogo | Creencia Previa Asumida por el Hablante |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Verb BE** | *Then, he **is** here!* | *I thought he was **not** here.* |
| **Modal** | *Then, you **can** ski!* | *I assumed you did **not** know how to ski.* |
| **Present DO/DOES** | *Then, you **do** play tennis!* | *I thought you did **not** play tennis.* |
| **Past DID** | *Then, you **did** pass!*<br>*Then, he **did** get a new car.* | *I thought you had **not** passed.*<br>*I thought he would **not** get a new car.* |
| **Perfect HAVE/HAS** | *So the mail **has** come already.* | *I assumed the mail had **not yet** arrived.* |

---

## 14. Expresiones de Deseo (*Wishes* — Skill 14)

Una oración con el verbo *wish* implica que la realidad fáctica es **exactamente lo opuesto**:

| Estructura del Deseo | Ejemplo | Realidad Verdadera |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wish + Pasado simple** | *I wish that he was/were here now.* | **He is not here now.** |
| **Wish + Pasado perfecto** | *I wish I had not signed that contract.* | **I did sign the contract.** |

## 16. Patrones de Error Gramatical Común (Peterson's Practice Test 4)

| Regla Gramatical | Uso Incorrecto (*Error*) | Corrección Estándar (*Correct Form*) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Verbos que rigen gerundio** | *One should always avoid **to change** lanes...* | *...avoid **changing** lanes...* (*avoid + V-ing*). |
| **Pleonosmo de sujeto (Pronombre redundante)** | *Their custom **it is** to name...* | *Their custom **is** to name...* |
| **Correlativo NOT ONLY ... BUT ALSO** | *...not only to conduct our business **but as well as to** visit...* | *...**but also to** visit...* |
| **Doble marca comparativa redundante** | *...was **more better** suited...* | *...was **better** suited...* |
| **Consistencia de pronombres indefinidos** | *...not only should **one** know..., but **you** should...* | *...but **one** should also...* |
| **Preposición fija con bored** | *Dr. Anderson is bored **of** it...* | *...is bored **with** it...* |
| **Preposición con depend** | *Jane's decision will depend largely **to** her ability...* | *...depend largely **on/upon** her ability...* |
| **Concordancia Sujeto-Verbo con frases preposicionales** | *A bouquet of tulips **were displayed**...* | *A bouquet ... **was displayed**...* (*bouquet* es singular). |
| **Tiempo pasado anterior a otro pasado** | *He **has been** the first violinist before he retired...* | *He **had been** the first violinist...* |
| **Cláusula temporal refiriéndose al futuro** | *...as soon as he **will retire** next year.* | *...as soon as he **retires** next year.* |
| **Cuantificadores Contable vs Incontable** | *The population has **little** opportunities...* | *...has **few** opportunities...* (*opportunities* es contable). |
| **Caso del pronombre relativo en cláusulas incrustadas** | *...the murderer, **whom** they feared would soon claim...* | *...**who** they feared would soon claim...* (*who* es sujeto de *would claim*). |
| **Subjuntivo / Pasado en deseos** | *I wish that he **was** here now.* | *I wish that he **were** here now.* |
| **Adverbio modificando participio pasado** | *A **common held** belief is...* | *A **commonly held** belief is...* |
| **Orden de palabras en preguntas indirectas** | *...to ask him **what courses should I take**.* | *...to ask him **what courses I should take**.* |
| **Paralelismo en comparaciones con gerundios** | *Learning ... is often easier **than to find**...* | *Learning ... is often easier **than finding**...* |
| **Modal perfecto de posibilidad en pasado** | *That fire could **of burned** down...* | *That fire could **have burned** down...* |
