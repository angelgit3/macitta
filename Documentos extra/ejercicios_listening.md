# TOEFL Preparation — Banco de Ejercicios de Listening Comprehension (Skills 4–14)

> **Propósito:** Banco estructurado de reactivos de práctica donde cada ejercicio incluye el **guion de audio (*Audio Script*)**, la pregunta del narrador con **alta variedad de enfoques** (*Who is the speaker / What will s/he do next / What does the speaker imply / What does the speaker mean / How does the speaker evaluate*), las **4 opciones de examen**, la **clave de respuesta** y el **análisis de pistas contextuales y combinaciones de habilidades**.

# SKILL 4: Draw Conclusions About Who, What, Where

### Ejercicio 01 (Deducción de Rol Profesional / *WHO*)
- **🎧 Audio Script:**
  - **(man)**: *Doctor Vance said that the porcelain crown on my upper molar has cracked and needs replacement.*
  - **(woman)**: *Let me pull up your dental charts and schedule you for a preliminary fitting with the dentist on Thursday.*
  - **(narrator)**: *Who is the woman most likely to be?*
- **📝 In Your Test Book:**
  - (A) A dental receptionist.
  - (B) An orthopedic surgeon.
  - (C) A retail pharmacist.
  - (D) A college biology instructor.
- **💡 Solución:** **(A) A dental receptionist.**
  - *Pistas Contextuales:* `porcelain crown on upper molar`, `dental charts`, `schedule fitting with the dentist`.

# SKILL 5: Listen for Who and What in Passives

### Ejercicio 11 (Transformación Activa ➔ Pasiva)
- **🎧 Audio Script:**
  - **(man)**: *Did the municipal council approve the funding for the new library wing?*
  - **(woman)**: *The mayor signed the authorization bill late yesterday evening.*
  - **(narrator)**: *What does the woman mean?*
- **📝 In Your Test Book:**
  - (A) The mayor was asked to sign the library contract.
  - (B) The authorization bill was signed by the mayor.
  - (C) The municipal council rejected the mayor's bill.
  - (D) The new library wing was built yesterday.
- **💡 Solución:** **(B) The authorization bill was signed by the mayor.**
  - *Estructura:* Diálogo activo (*The mayor signed the bill*) ➔ Respuesta pasiva equivalente (*The bill was signed by the mayor*).

# SKILL 6: Listen for Who and What with Multiple Nouns

### Ejercicio 21 (Sustitución de Roles / *Stepped in to replace*)
- **🎧 Audio Script:**
  - **(man)**: *Who is directing the campus chamber orchestra for the winter concert?*
  - **(woman)**: *Professor Martinez stepped in to replace Maestro Rossi after he fell ill.*
  - **(narrator)**: *What does the woman say about the concert?*
- **📝 In Your Test Book:**
  - (A) Maestro Rossi is conducting the winter concert.
  - (B) Professor Martinez is directing the orchestra in place of Maestro Rossi.
  - (C) Maestro Rossi replaced Professor Martinez in the music department.
  - (D) Both conductors will share the podium for the concert.
- **💡 Solución:** **(B) Professor Martinez is directing the orchestra in place of Maestro Rossi.**
  - *Análisis:* *Martinez stepped in to replace Rossi* = Martinez asume la dirección; Rossi no participa.

  - **(narrator)**: *What does the man imply?*

  - (B) The supervisor conducted the second titration test himself.

- **💡 Solución:** **(C) Greg was required by his supervisor to perform the test again.**
  - *Análisis:* *Supervisor made Greg repeat* = El supervisor obligó a Greg a repetir la prueba.

# TOEFL CUMULATIVE REVIEW: Multi-Skill Integration Exercises (Skills 4–14)

> **Enfoque Pedagógico:** En estas conversaciones acumulativas se integran **múltiples habilidades de forma simultánea**, simulando el nivel de complejidad del examen TOEFL real donde una sola oración puede involucrar voz pasiva, negación, deducción de rol e inversión semántica.

---

### Ejercicio 79 (Integración: Skills 4, 7 & 10 — Rol + Negación + Comparativo)
- **🎧 Audio Script:**
  - **(man)**: *The defendant claims he didn't drive recklessly through the school zone, but the radar records show he was traveling at twice the speed limit.*
  - **(woman)**: *No witness was more credible than the traffic officer who testified this morning, counselor.*
  - **(narrator)**: *Who is the woman, and what does she imply about the testimony?*
- **📝 In Your Test Book:**
  - (A) A defense lawyer who believes the radar equipment was faulty.
  - (B) A presiding judge who found the officer's testimony the most convincing.
  - (C) A police officer who observed the speeding vehicle.
  - (D) A court clerk who doubted the traffic officer's evidence.
- **💡 Solución:** **(B) A presiding judge who found the officer's testimony the most convincing.**
  - **🧠 Análisis Multi-Habilidad:**
    - **Skill 4 (*Who*):** Vocabulario judicial (*defendant*, *counselor*, *testified*, *witness*) revela que la mujer es la jueza que preside la corte.
    - **Skill 10 (*Neg + Comp*):** *No witness was more credible* equivale al superlativo absoluto: el testimonio del oficial fue el más creíble y convincente de todos.

### Ejercicio 80 (Integración: Skills 5, 8 & 11 — Pasiva + Doble Negación + Acuerdo)
- **🎧 Audio Script:**
  - **(woman)**: *It's not impossible that the architectural blueprints were leaked by an insider before the bidding deadline.*
  - **(man)**: *You can say that again!*
  - **(narrator)**: *What does the man mean?*
- **📝 In Your Test Book:**
  - (A) He wants the woman to repeat her statement about the deadline.
  - (B) He agrees that an insider might have distributed the blueprints.
  - (C) He thinks it is impossible for the blueprints to be released.
  - (D) He denies that the bidding deadline was postponed.
- **💡 Solución:** **(B) He agrees that an insider might have distributed the blueprints.**
  - **🧠 Análisis Multi-Habilidad:**
    - **Skill 8 (*Double Negative*):** *not impossible* = *possible / might have happened*.
    - **Skill 5 (*Passive ➔ Active*):** *blueprints were leaked by an insider* = *an insider distributed the plans*.
    - **Skill 11 (*Agreement*):** *You can say that again!* = coincide plenamente con la posibilidad del robo de planos.
